Home Upkeep: Maintenance Log for Android

Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

This policy applies only to the Home Upkeep: Maintenance Log Android app (package com.siditude.homeupkeeplog) by Technical Dost Solutions. It does not cover other Technical Dost apps or the website.

Records you enter

The app saves your maintenance tasks, due dates, notes, optional costs and completion history in its private storage on your device. We do not require an account and do not upload these records to our servers. Task tracking and history work offline.

There is no automatic cloud backup. You can export a CSV copy, or, with Plus, save a PDF history report to a destination you choose. That destination handles the copy under its own privacy practices. Importing a compatible CSV replaces local tasks and history only after you confirm. Uninstalling the app deletes its local data; we cannot recover it from a server.

Reminders

With your permission, the app sends local notifications for due-date reminders. If you decline, tasks remain visible in the app. Android power settings may delay reminders. The app may receive a boot-completed signal to reschedule local reminders after a restart.

Advertising and privacy choices

The free plan may show ads supplied by Google Mobile Ads when internet access is available. Depending on your settings and location, Google's advertising services may collect or share IP address, app interactions, diagnostics and device or account identifiers for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. We do not send your maintenance task records to Google Mobile Ads or sell those records.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform asks for your ad privacy choices. You can reopen available privacy choices from the app's About screen. Read Google's privacy policy and its Mobile Ads data disclosure guidance.

Optional Plus subscription

Google Play Billing processes Plus purchases and checks subscription status. Google Play handles payment and purchase records under Google's privacy practices; the app uses the purchase result to enable Plus features and remove ads. We do not attach your maintenance records to purchase checks. An internet connection is needed to verify Plus status. You can manage or cancel Plus in Google Play. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

Your choices and contact

You can edit or delete tasks and history in the app, export records before changing devices, decline notification permission and change available ad privacy choices. This app is not directed to children.

For privacy questions, contact support@technicaldost.com. We will update this policy and its effective date if the app's practices change.
